Pocket PC Privacy Policy

App: Pocket PC (com.pocketmouse.app) · Controller: Muñoz Studios (Diego Pineda)

Pocket PC turns your phone into a mouse, keyboard and remote control for your computer. Your commands travel straight to your device, not through our servers.

At a glance

  • Your movements, clicks and what you type travel straight to your computer, over Wi-Fi, USB or Bluetooth. We have no servers that receive them.
  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name or email.
  • We don't sell your information. The free version shows Google AdMob ads; with Pocket PC Premium, none.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Who is responsible

Pocket PC is an app by Muñoz Studios (identifier com.pocketmouse.app), Diego Pineda's software studio. It consists of the Android app and the free Pocket PC program for Windows. Muñoz Studios is responsible for the information Pocket PC handles under this policy.

For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to us at diegoeduardopineda@gmail.com.

How it connects to your computer

  • Bluetooth: the phone presents itself as a mouse and keyboard to the device you choose. Nothing needs to be installed on that device and the connection does not go through the internet.
  • Wi-Fi or USB: the phone connects to the Pocket PC program you install on your Windows PC, within your local network or over a cable. That communication does not go through the internet or through our servers.

To remember that you already authorized a phone, the app stores a random identifier it creates on your phone, and the Windows program stores on your PC the list of authorized phones: each phone's name and model, that identifier and a secret key unique to each phone, which the phone uses to prove it is really that phone every time it connects. The app also remembers the PCs you connected to (their name and their address on your network).

To connect a new phone, the Windows program shows a single-use QR code that expires after 10 minutes. If your PC has internet, the code is shown on a page of our website (dprojectlab.com): the code's data (your PC's address on your network, its name and the code) goes after the "#" sign of the address, a part your browser never sends to the server. The app reads the code with the Google Play services code scanner, which processes the image on your phone; the app does not ask for camera permission.

What travels between your phone and your computer

  • From your phone to your computer: touchpad movements, clicks, gestures, keys, the text you type, your shortcuts and device commands (volume, media, presentations, lock or shut down).
  • From your computer to your phone, to show it in the app: the PC's name, processor and memory usage, battery, uptime and, if something is playing, its title, artist, artwork and status.

None of this goes through our servers: we do not store it or see it. Air mode uses the phone's gyroscope only to move the cursor or the laser: it is processed on the spot and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Screen mirroring

If you open the Screen tab, the Windows program captures the image of the monitor you choose and streams it live to your phone over your local network, so you can see and control your PC from it. The image is not recorded or stored anywhere, and it stops being sent when you close that tab or turn mirroring off. Keep in mind that your phone will show everything on that screen, including private messages or data.

Presentations

With a PowerPoint presentation open, the Windows program sends your phone the slide thumbnails, their speaker notes and the slide number, so you can see them while you present. Thumbnails pass only for an instant through your PC's temporary folder and are deleted right away. If you edit a note from your phone, the change is saved in your presentation, on your PC. The laser pointer is drawn on your screen and is not recorded.

File transfer

If you send a file from your phone to your PC, the Windows program saves it in your Downloads folder or in the folder you choose. If you send a file from your PC to your phone, the app saves it in the phone's Downloads folder or in the one you choose, and lets you know with a notification. Everything happens within your local network.

The app only accesses the files you choose or share with it: it does not have permission to browse your gallery or your files.

Shared clipboard

With the shared clipboard, text you copy on your PC reaches your phone so you can paste it there, and the keyboard's "Paste" button types on your PC what you copied on your phone. That text only travels between your phone and your PC, over your local network: it does not go through our servers and we do not store it. It is on by default and you can turn it off in Settings › Shared clipboard.

Actions on your computer

Locking, sleeping, restarting and shutting down the PC only happen when you ask for it from the app and confirm it. Restarting or shutting down may close programs with unsaved work: save it first.

Ads (free version)

The free version of the app shows Google AdMob ads: a banner, some full-screen ads and, if you choose to, videos that give you ad-free hours. To show them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad service may collect:

  • Your phone's advertising ID and other device or installation identifiers.
  • An approximate location derived from your IP address (Pocket PC does not ask for access to your location).
  • How you interact with the ads, for example whether they were shown or whether you tapped them.
  • Technical and diagnostic information, such as the phone model and the app's performance.

Pocket PC never shares with Google or advertisers what you type, what you copy, your files, your screen image or anything you do on your computer. Your options:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks you first whether you accept personalized ads. There, and wherever privacy laws require it, you can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy options.
  • In any country you can reset or delete the advertising ID from your phone settings (on most phones: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Pocket PC Premium shows no ads. The Windows program never shows ads.

More information about how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Pocket PC Premium purchases

If you buy Pocket PC Premium, as a subscription or with a one-time payment, the payment is processed by Google Play. Pocket PC does not see or store your payment details: it only stores on your phone that you have Premium. Subscriptions are managed and canceled from Google Play. The name you engrave on your medal is stored only on your phone and only leaves it if you choose to share the medal image.

The Windows program

The Windows program works within your local network. It only connects to the internet to open pages of our website that you ask for (the QR code to connect a phone, and help). As with any website, when you open a page our hosting provider (Cloudflare) receives technical data such as your IP address and your browser, which are used to serve the page and protect it from attacks. The program has no ads and no analytics tools.

On your PC it stores the list of authorized phones, your preferences, the files you receive and a startup log (when it opened and how long it took to be ready, in %APPDATA%\PocketPC\arranque.log) that is only used to diagnose slow connections and never leaves your PC. If you install Pocket PC from the Microsoft Store, the download and updates are handled by Microsoft under its own privacy policy.

If you write to us

If you contact support from the app (Settings › Contact support), your email app opens with a message that includes technical information (app version, phone brand and model, Android version and connection type) to help us solve your case. You see that text before sending it and can remove it. We use your email only to reply to you.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name or email.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • We have no servers of our own that receive or store what you type, copy, send or see on your screen.
  • We don't use analytics tools of our own: the only data that leaves your phone for third parties is what Google receives for ads and purchases, described above.
  • We don't use the camera or the microphone, and we don't ask for access to your location, contacts, SMS or gallery.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

What Pocket PC stores lives on your devices for as long as you keep it: on your phone, your preferences, your shortcuts, your medal name, the PCs you connected to and the random identifier; on your PC, the list of authorized phones with their keys, your preferences and the startup log.

  • Uninstall the app to delete everything it stores on the phone.
  • Uninstall the Windows program and delete the %APPDATA%\PocketPC folder to delete what it stores on your PC, including the list of authorized phones.
  • The files you received stay in the folder you chose until you delete them.

The data Google receives for ads and purchases is kept and managed by Google under its own policy. We keep the emails you send us only as long as needed to help you.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you have the right to:

  • Know what data is handled and get a copy: this policy explains it, and almost everything is visible in the app.
  • Correct or delete it: you can do it yourself from the app or by uninstalling it, as explained above.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, for example to personalized ads or to the shared clipboard. What was handled before you withdrew it is not affected.
  • Object to a use of your data or ask for it to be restricted.
  • File a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.

Since Pocket PC does not keep your data on our servers, you can do almost everything yourself. For anything else write to us at diegoeduardopineda@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days at most. Exercising your rights is free and you will not be treated differently for doing so.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, these are the legal reasons your information is processed:

  • Your consent: personalized ads. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Our legitimate interest in the app working well and being secure: non-personalized ads, fraud prevention and the startup log.

Pocket PC does not send your data to any server of its own.

We don't sell your information. You have the right to know what data is handled, to correct it and to delete it, and you will not be treated differently for exercising these rights.

If you live in Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Peru, Chile or another country with data protection laws, you have the rights your law grants you, such as access, rectification, deletion and objection, and you can exercise them as explained in "Your rights".

Security

The Windows program only accepts phones you authorized, and each phone must prove with its secret key that it is really that phone. Screen mirroring and file transfer only work within the session opened by an authorized phone. Purchases and ads use Google's encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Communication between your phone and your PC travels within your local network and is not end-to-end encrypted. Use it on trusted networks, such as your home or office network, and authorize only your own devices: on a public network, other people connected to the same network could try to see that traffic.

No system is infallible. If we detect a security issue affecting your data, we will fix it and, where the law requires it, notify you through the means available to us, such as an app update or this website.

Pocket PC is intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, or the age set by the law of your country, and it does not knowingly collect information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, write to us at diegoeduardopineda@gmail.com.
