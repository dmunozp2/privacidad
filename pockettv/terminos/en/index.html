What happens on your TV

The commands you send from the phone run on your TV as if you used its own remote.

  • The power button can turn off the TV, and opening an app or switching inputs interrupts what was playing.
  • A tap or a key can land somewhere else on the screen if the TV changed what it shows. Check the TV before sending important commands, such as buying or deleting something inside another app.
  • The accessibility service is optional and you turn it on in the TV settings. With it, the phone can control any app on the TV; you can turn it off at any time.
  • On the TV, the app opens by itself when the TV turns on so the receiver is ready. You can remove everything by uninstalling it.
