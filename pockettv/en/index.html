Pocket TV Privacy Policy

App: Pocket TV (com.munozstudios.pockettv) · Controller: Muñoz Studios (Diego Pineda)

Pocket TV turns your phone into the remote control for your Android TV. Your commands travel over your own network, not through our servers.

At a glance

  • Your commands, photos, videos and the screen you cast travel straight from your phone to your TV, inside your Wi-Fi network. We have no servers that receive them.
  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name or email.
  • We don't sell your information. On the phone, the free version shows Google AdMob ads; with Pocket TV Premium, none. There are never ads on the TV.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

One app for the phone and the TV

Pocket TV is the same app on the phone and on the TV. On a phone or tablet it works as a remote control; on a TV or box with Android TV or Google TV it works as a receiver that gets and shows what you send. The app picks the mode based on the type of device. This policy covers both.

How your phone and TV connect

The phone and the TV talk directly over your Wi-Fi network. Nothing goes through our servers: we have no servers that receive your commands, photos or videos. The TV only accepts connections from devices on your own local network.

The phone looks for your TV on the local network and you pair it once with the 4-digit code shown on the TV screen. When paired, the TV gives the phone its own secret key; from then on, every time it connects, the phone proves it is itself with that key, without sending it over the network. The phone stores the TVs you paired (their name and address on your network) and the TV stores the name and key of each authorized phone.

What travels between the phone and the TV

  • From the phone to the TV: remote keys, touchpad movements, the text you type with the keyboard, the links you open on the TV, the photos and videos you send and your screen image if you cast it.
  • From the TV to the phone, to show it in the app: the TV name, model and version, what is playing (title, position and audio and subtitle tracks), the list of installed apps and inputs on the TV, and whether the accessibility service is on.

None of this goes through our servers: we don't store it or see it.

Photos, videos and screen casting

  • Photos and videos: you choose what to send with the Android file picker. The app only accesses what you choose and has no permission to browse your gallery. While sending, the phone makes the file available to your TV, inside your network and with a random link, and stops when it's done. The app remembers on your phone where you left a video so you can resume it.
  • Screen casting: uses Android screen capture, which asks for your confirmation every time. Your screen shows on the TV in real time and is not recorded or saved. It stops when you lock the phone (you can change this in Settings › Privacy). If you want, the app can silence notifications while casting so they don't appear on the TV; that requires an Android permission ("Do Not Disturb" access) that you grant.

Keep in mind that everything you send or cast shows on the TV screen, where anyone nearby can see it.

Built-in browser

The phone browser opens pages directly from the internet, like any other browser: the sites you visit can see your IP address and store cookies. History, bookmarks and site icons are stored only on your phone, and icons are requested directly from each site. You can clear history and cookies in Settings › Browser.

The browser ad blocker runs inside your phone and sends data to no one. When you send a video or a page to your TV, the TV downloads it directly from that site, which can see your network's IP address.

Accessibility service on the TV (optional)

On the TV, Pocket TV offers an optional accessibility service. Before you turn it on, the app explains what it is for and asks for your confirmation; then you turn it on yourself in the TV settings. With it, your phone can move a cursor, use the arrows, go home or back, swipe and type text in any app on the TV.

To do this, the service needs to know which element has focus on the screen, to press it or type into it. It does not read, store or send the content of what appears on your screen. It also keeps the receiver ready when you turn on the TV and brings Pocket TV to the front when you send a video or photo. We don't use it for anything else. You can turn it off at any time in the TV accessibility settings.

Ads (free phone version)

On the phone, the free version shows Google AdMob ads: a banner, full-screen ads when you return to the app and, if you choose, rewarded videos (watching 4 short videos gives you 24 hours without ads). To show them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad service may collect:

  • Your phone's advertising ID and other device or installation identifiers.
  • An approximate location based on your IP address (Pocket TV does not ask for access to your location).
  • How you interact with the ads, for example whether they were shown or whether you tapped them.
  • Technical and diagnostic information, such as the phone model and the app's performance.

Pocket TV never shares with Google or advertisers what you type, what you send to the TV, your screen image or what you watch. Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks first whether you accept personalized ads. There, and wherever privacy laws require it, you can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy.
  • In any country you can reset or delete the advertising ID from your phone settings (on most phones: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • With Pocket TV Premium no ads are shown. There are never ads on the TV.

More information about how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Crash reports

If the app closes because of an error, on the phone or on the TV, it sends a technical report to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so we can find and fix the problem. The report includes the app version, the Android version, the device brand and model, the technical details of the error and an installation identifier. It does not include your photos, videos, history, what you type or what you see on screen. The phone also keeps a copy of the last error, which you can share with us by email if you want.

Pocket TV Premium purchases

Pocket TV Premium is a yearly subscription bought on Google Play, which may include a free trial. Payment is processed by Google Play: Pocket TV does not see or store your payment details and only keeps on your phone the fact that you have Premium. The subscription is managed and canceled from Google Play. The name you engrave on your medal is stored only on your phone and only leaves it if you choose to share the medal image.

If you write to us

If you contact support from the app (Settings › About › Contact support), your email app opens with a message that includes technical information (app version, phone brand and model, Android version, whether you are connected and your TV model and version) to help us solve your case. You see that text before sending it and can remove it. We use your email only to reply to you.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name or email.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • We have no servers of our own that receive or store what you send, type or watch.
  • We don't use analytics tools of our own: the only data that leaves your phone or TV for third parties is what Google and Firebase receive for ads, purchases and crash reports, described above.
  • We don't use the camera or the microphone, and we don't ask for access to your location, contacts, SMS or gallery.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

What Pocket TV keeps lives on your devices for as long as you keep it: on your phone, your paired TVs, your settings, browser history and bookmarks, where you left a video and your medal name; on your TV, the list of authorized phones with their keys.

  • You can clear history and cookies in Settings › Browser, and remove a TV from Settings.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything it keeps on the phone or on the TV.

The data Google receives for ads, purchases and crash reports is kept and managed by Google under its own policy. We keep the emails you send us only as long as needed to help you.

Security

The TV only accepts phones paired with the code it shows on screen, and each phone must prove with its secret key that it is itself. After several wrong codes, the TV changes the code and pauses pairing. It only accepts devices on your local network and one phone at a time, and screen casting is only accepted with a one-time pass from the session opened by an authorized phone. Purchases and ads use Google's encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Communication between your phone and your TV travels inside your local network and is not end-to-end encrypted. Use it on trusted networks, like your home network, and don't share your TV's code: on a public network, other people on the same network could try to see that traffic.

No system is infallible. If we detect a security issue affecting your data, we will fix it and, where the law requires it, notify you through the means available to us, such as an app update or this website.
